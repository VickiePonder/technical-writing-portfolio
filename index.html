Featured case study / 2026

From weekly reporting friction
to a standardized PMO operating model.

A portfolio manager spent approximately 35 hours each week chasing status updates, transferring Jira data to spreadsheets, and emailing a weekly snapshot to the CIO. I designed and implemented a more connected process spanning project intake, approval, provisioning, documentation standards, and dashboard visibility.

~$100MPortfolio value
60+Projects
~40Project managers
35 hrs/weekFormer manual reporting workload

The 35-hour figure represents the previous weekly workload, not independently verified time saved.

The challenge

Fragmented, manual, and stale

Updates depended on repeated follow-ups with approximately 40 PMs. Leadership received a static weekly report instead of a routinely refreshed portfolio view.

The solution

One process, consistent standards

Forms-driven intake, director approval routing, templated project workspaces, standardized Jira data, and Power BI embedded in SharePoint with multiple daily refreshes.

02 / RIOT GAMES · GLOBAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Global Data Center Transformation & Infrastructure Automation

Challenge. Coordinating international infrastructure decommissioning while maintaining capacity visibility, operational reporting, standardized facility records, and audit support.

Solution. Oversaw the decommissioning of 36 data centers across LATAM, APAC, and Europe in nine months. Supported infrastructure capacity administration, automated reporting and notifications, standardized data center floor plans, and contributed to data center audits and cloud/FinOps initiatives.

Outcome. Automated reporting and notifications helped reduce the down-server count from dozens to one, while standardized records supported consistent infrastructure operations.

36 data centers9 monthsDozens → 1 down serversCloud & FinOps
03 / HEARST · BUSINESS CONTINUITY

Enterprise BCDR Documentation Transformation

Challenge. Business continuity and disaster recovery knowledge was fragmented across approximately 12 geographically dispersed teams, with inconsistent document structures and access processes.

Solution. Developed and centralized BCDR documentation, created standardized templates, organized recovery knowledge, and defined consistent document access processes to help distributed teams maintain usable operational guidance.

Outcome. Established a shared, repeatable documentation framework for recovery planning and access across distributed teams, improving consistency and discoverability.

~12 teamsCentralized knowledgeStandard templatesAccess governance
04 / PWC VIA MBO PARTNERS · KNOWLEDGE MIGRATION

Enterprise Knowledge Migration & SharePoint Adoption

Challenge. Documentation for 18 teams was distributed across Confluence and Google Drive, requiring a unified platform and a sustainable content-management model.

Solution. Migrated content to SharePoint; built a standardized communication site and 18 team sites; introduced reusable SharePoint page and list templates; trained teams; and conducted office hours to support adoption.

Outcome. Created a consistent SharePoint knowledge environment and enabled teams to create and maintain their own content using shared standards.

18 team sites1 communication hubConfluence + Google DriveTraining & office hours
