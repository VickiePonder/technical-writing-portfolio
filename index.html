02 / RIOT GAMES · GLOBAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Global Data Center Transformation & Infrastructure Automation

36Data centers
9 monthsDecommissioning period
Dozens → 1Down-server count
3 regionsLATAM · APAC · Europe

The challenge. Coordinating international infrastructure decommissioning while maintaining capacity visibility, operational reporting, standardized facility records, and audit support.

Scope of work. Oversaw the decommissioning of 36 data centers across LATAM, APAC, and Europe in nine months. Supported infrastructure capacity administration, automated reporting and notifications, standardized data center floor plans, and contributed to data center audits and cloud/FinOps initiatives.

Impact. Automated reporting and notifications helped reduce the down-server count from dozens to one, while standardized records supported consistent infrastructure operations.

04 / PWC VIA MBO PARTNERS · KNOWLEDGE MIGRATION

Enterprise Knowledge Migration & SharePoint Adoption

18SharePoint team sites
1Communication hub
2 → 1Source platforms to destination
18 teamsTraining & adoption

The challenge. Documentation for 18 teams was distributed across Confluence and Google Drive, requiring a unified platform and a sustainable content-management model.

Scope of work. Migrated content to SharePoint; built a standardized communication site and 18 team sites; introduced reusable SharePoint page and list templates; trained teams; and conducted office hours to support adoption.

Impact. Created a consistent SharePoint knowledge environment and enabled teams to create and maintain their own content using shared standards.
